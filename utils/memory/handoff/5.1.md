- **Dynamic Frame Formatting**:
  - Refactored `spi_injector_build_frame` in `esp32/main/spi_injector_master.c` to format dynamic payload frames matching `cora7z/app/sw/src/axi_spi_receiver.c`:

    - Payload buffer: `8 .. 8 + len - 1`.
